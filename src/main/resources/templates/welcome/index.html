Welcome to EventHub

Welcome to EventHub - your new all-in-one campus event management system! EventHub makes it easy for students to discover, RSVP, and keep track of upcoming events, while giving clubs and organizers the tools to create, publish, and manage their activities. Whether you're looking for academic talks, music nights, sports competitions, or club fairs, EventHub brings the whole campus community together in one place.

With features like event search and filters, personalized recommendations, RSVP tracking, and photo galleries, EventHub ensures you never miss out on what's happening around campus.
